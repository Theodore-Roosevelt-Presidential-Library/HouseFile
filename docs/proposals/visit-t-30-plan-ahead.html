Your Visit Day

Your date and entry time are on your ticket confirmation. The Library's hours change with the season, so check what time the doors open and close that day before you plan the rest of it. Salt+Scoria is open when the Library is, and no admission ticket is needed to eat there.
Hours for Your Visit
Coming between late fall and spring? Snow and high winds can close I-94 in western North Dakota. Check road conditions before you set out and give yourself extra time.
