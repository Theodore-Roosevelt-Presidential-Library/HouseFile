What Membership Brings Back

  • Free general admission for the people your level covers, and 25% off extra admission tickets
  • The member line at the entrance and dedicated member support
  • 10% off in the Library Shop, online and onsite, and at Salt+Scoria (excluding alcohol)
  • Priority access to select ticketed events and discounted tickets for programs and lectures
  • Member tours, exhibition previews and member events
  • 10% off gift memberships, and the monthly member e-news
Membership also supports the Library's educational programming, conservation education and the preservation of Theodore Roosevelt's legacy.
 

How to Rejoin

  1. Have your membership number ready. It is at the top of this email.
  2. Select Renew My Membership and verify with your membership number and the primary cardholder's last name.
  3. Choose your level and term: 1 year, auto-renew or 2 years. Your membership number stays the same.
Questions? Write to membership@trlibrary.com.
