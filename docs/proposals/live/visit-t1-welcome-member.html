Four Things to Plan Now

Where to Stay

Medora has limited lodging, and summer weekends book up fast. See hotels, cabins, rentals and campgrounds with room on your dates, all in one place.

Getting Here

Medora sits on I-94: Exit 24 from the west, Exit 27 from the east. A rental car is the way to see the Badlands. Coverage is spotty, so download your route before you go.
Directions

Plan Your Days

Allow about three and a half hours for the galleries, plus time for the boardwalk and the rooftop prairie. The Trip Planner builds a day-by-day route around your interests.

The National Park

Theodore Roosevelt National Park's South Unit entrance is right in town. A favorite plan: the scenic loop in the morning, the Library in the afternoon.
