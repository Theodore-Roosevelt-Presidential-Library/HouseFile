Three Days to the Badlands

In three days, you will stand where a young Theodore Roosevelt rebuilt himself, on the buttes above the Little Missouri. Here is everything that makes the day go smoothly.
Thinking about membership? Anyone at the front desk can refund these tickets and put what you paid toward a membership, and your reservation stays as it is. You can also email membership@trlibrary.com before you come.

Your Entry Window

Arrive up to 15 minutes before the time on your ticket, or any time within that hour. With a 10:00 AM ticket, come any time from 9:45 to 11:00 AM. Leave a few minutes for the walk from the lot and a quick security check.

Only service animals go inside; leashed dogs are welcome on the grounds. Never leave a pet in a parked car.

Parking

Parking is free. Take the first roundabout into the main lot under the solar canopies. Driving an RV or towing? Use the Medora Musical lot instead. Accessible spaces and a drop-off sit right by the entrance, and EV chargers are first come, first served.

Make a Day of It

Plan about three and a half hours for the galleries, then walk the one-mile boardwalk or the rooftop prairie. Many visitors pair the Library with the national park: drive the South Unit's scenic loop in the morning, when wildlife is most active, and spend the afternoon here. Salt+Scoria serves breakfast and lunch, and it is quieter before or after the noon rush.
