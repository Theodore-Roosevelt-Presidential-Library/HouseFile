For the Teacher in Your Life

Teach with TR is the Library's collection of classroom resources for K-12: lesson plans built on North Dakota and national standards, primary-source packets on the Rough Riders, the Pure Food and Drug Act, women's suffrage and more, timelines (one written just for kids), and hands-on activities, from designing a cattle brand to making T.R.'s own frying pan bread.
